About me

As an ICF Professional Certified Coach, I have worked with senior executives, entrepreneurs, and high-potential leaders across multinational corporations and fast-growing startups for more than 15 years. I have supported over 1,000 professionals worldwide, including MBA students, alumni, and staff at the Wharton School.

My perspective is shaped by experience as both a coach and an entrepreneur. Having founded and scaled an executive search firm and a digital mental health technology company, I bring firsthand insight into leadership, organizational growth, talent strategy, and the realities of building businesses in fast-changing global environments.

As a researcher, my work at the intersection of psychology and artificial intelligence informs how I think about decision-making, behavior change, coaching, and human well-being.
