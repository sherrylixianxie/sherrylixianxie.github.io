Psychology meets AI.

I am a PhD student in Social & Organizational Psychology at Teachers College, Columbia University. My research brings together positive psychology, AI, and coaching to support human flourishing.

I work with Dr. James Westaby in the Dynamic Network Lab at Teachers College, Dr. Michael Morris in the Morris Lab at Columbia Business School, and Dr. Gabriele Oettingen in the Motivation Lab at NYU.

Publications

Comparing the Effectiveness of LLM-Powered Coaching with Human Coaching and GPT Conversation

The Journal of Positive Psychology · 2025

A randomized controlled study comparing a purpose-built LLM coach, human coaching, and GPT-4. The AI coach performed comparably to human coaching across measured dimensions and exceeded general GPT-4 conversation in empathy, engagement, and encouragement.

The Application of Large Language Models in Coaching

International Coaching Psychology Review · 2025

This paper examines people’s receptivity to AI coaching and introduces a knowledge-based framework that integrates coaching models, psychological theory, and prompt engineering to support personalized and scalable AI-driven coaching.
